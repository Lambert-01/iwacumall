Delete your IwacuMall account

You can request deletion of your IwacuMall shopping account and associated personal information. This page is public and does not require sign-in.

Delete from the app or website

  1. In the Android app, open Account. On the website, open your account.
  2. Choose Delete my account, type DELETE, then confirm with your password or the Google account linked to your profile.

If you cannot sign in

Email iwacumallltd@gmail.com with the subject IwacuMall account deletion request. Send the request from the email address on the account when possible. If you no longer have access to it, contact us by phone at +250 788 651 421 so we can explain how to verify the request safely. Never send a password, card number, bank password, or mobile-money PIN.

What is deleted

After we verify and process a deletion, account credentials, the profile, saved delivery addresses, active sign-in sessions, verification and password-reset tokens, and account-linked checkout retry records are removed or anonymized. You cannot sign in again with the deleted account.

Orders that are still being paid, delivered, returned, refunded, or otherwise handled may temporarily retain the contact details needed to complete that transaction. They are detached from your account. Once handling is complete and the applicable return period has ended, direct contact details and identifying order-email copies are erased. Where an order record is subject to Rwanda tax-record requirements, a minimized accounting record is kept for ten years starting on 1 January following the relevant fiscal year; the system currently uses the calendar fiscal year. It is then deleted along with its stored order audit and email copies. See the Privacy Policy for details.
