Information used to fulfil orders

When you register or order, IwacuMall processes account details such as your name and email, and order details such as your phone number, delivery address, purchased items, invoice totals, payment status and any payment reference submitted to the store.

Why it is used

The store uses this information to manage your account, prepare and deliver orders, issue invoices, provide support, confirm manual invoice payments and protect the service from misuse. Passwords are stored as password hashes. Browser sessions use an HttpOnly session cookie; native app sessions use secure device storage.

Payments and service providers

IwacuMall currently provides manual payment instructions after checkout. This website does not collect card details or confirm transfers automatically. The store may use its configured hosting, MongoDB Atlas and email delivery providers to operate the service.
