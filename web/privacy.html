Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy applies to the IwacuMall shopping app for Android and the IwacuMall website. It explains what information is handled when you browse, create an account, place an order, or contact us.

Who operates IwacuMall

IwacuMall is operated from Downtown Kigali, Rwanda. For privacy questions or requests, contact iwacumallltd@gmail.com or call +250 788 651 421.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, password verifier (we store a password hash, not your readable password), account identifiers, email-verification state, and account security tokens. If you choose Google sign-in, Google supplies an identity token and the account name, email address, and Google account identifier needed to sign you in.
  • Delivery and order information: recipient name, phone number, city, delivery address, delivery note, products and variants purchased, quantities, invoice number, totals, selected payment method, payment status, and any payment or refund reference recorded for the order.
  • Saved addresses and support information: address labels and delivery details you save, plus information you include in a return request or send to customer support.
  • App and security information: a sign-in session identifier, requests needed to load the catalog and complete shopping features, and limited security rate-limit records. The server stores session tokens as hashes. Rate-limit records use a keyed, non-reversible value derived from a network address and expire automatically.
  • Information on your device: the app keeps your cart and a small cached account profile in local app storage. The sign-in token is kept in Android secure storage. The app requests internet access; it does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or phone-call permissions.

How we use information

  • Show products, categories, cart contents, and checkout features.
  • Create and protect customer accounts, support Google sign-in, verify email addresses, and handle password resets.
  • Prepare, deliver, track, and support orders; issue invoices; confirm manual payments; and handle returns, refunds, and customer questions.
  • Send order and invoice messages to the email address entered at checkout, and send the store a copy needed to process the order.
  • Protect the service, prevent misuse, troubleshoot errors, and keep the business records needed for transactions and applicable obligations.

Payments

IwacuMall uses invoice-based payment instructions. The app does not ask for or store payment-card numbers, online-banking passwords, or mobile-money PINs. You complete payment with the payment provider shown in the invoice. IwacuMall records the chosen payment method and payment status, and may record a transaction reference when the store confirms a payment or refund.

Who receives information

We do not sell personal information. We share only what is needed to operate the store and fulfil your requests:

  • IwacuMall staff: authorized store staff can access customer and order details needed to process delivery, payment confirmation, returns, and support.
  • MongoDB Atlas: stores account, order, and application records for the IwacuMall service.
  • Contabo hosting: hosts the IwacuMall website and API. Server and network operations may process technical connection data such as an IP address, requested URL, and timestamps.
  • Google: processes sign-in when you choose Google sign-in. IwacuMall receives the identity information needed to authenticate your account.
  • Cloudinary: stores and delivers product and category images uploaded by store administrators. Customers are not asked to upload profile photos.
  • Email delivery provider: currently Gmail SMTP delivers verification, password-reset, order, and invoice messages. Those messages may contain your email address, order details, and invoice or delivery information. Order messages are addressed to you and to the store inbox.
  • Payment providers: when you pay using the external bank, mobile-money, or other provider listed in your invoice, that provider processes your payment under its own privacy terms.

These providers may process information in countries outside Rwanda. Their processing locations depend on their service and account configuration.

Retention and account deletion

We keep account information while your account is active, including order history linked to your account so you can review past purchases and invoices. If you delete your account, we remove the profile, saved addresses, sign-in sessions, verification and reset tokens, and account-linked checkout retry records. The account can no longer be used to sign in.

For an order that is still being paid, delivered, returned, refunded, or otherwise being handled, we temporarily retain the contact details needed to complete that transaction and unlink the order from the deleted account. Once the order is closed and its applicable return period has ended, we erase direct customer contact details from the order, stored email copies, and order audit details. We retain a minimized transaction record (such as items, invoice number, totals, payment and fulfilment status, and dates) for applicable accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, and dispute purposes. Where the record is subject to Rwanda's tax-record keeping requirements, the statutory period is ten years starting on 1 January after the relevant fiscal year. The system currently uses the calendar fiscal year for this schedule. After the retention period, the minimized order record and its stored audit/email copies are deleted. Copies already delivered to an email recipient cannot be recalled from that recipient's mailbox.

To delete an account in the app, open Account → Delete my account. You can also use the public account deletion instructions. If you cannot sign in, email us from the address associated with the account; we may ask for information needed to verify the request. Do not email your password, card number, bank password, or mobile-money PIN.

Cookies and security

The website uses an HttpOnly sign-in session cookie and a separate CSRF protection cookie when needed. The Android app stores its sign-in token in Android secure storage. We use HTTPS for network connections, hash passwords and server-side session tokens, and restrict order access to authorized accounts and staff. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so protect your sign-in details and contact us if you suspect misuse.

Children

IwacuMall is a general shopping service and is not designed to collect account information directly from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information can contact us to request its removal.

Your choices and requests

You may ask to access or correct your information, delete your account, or raise a privacy concern using the contact details below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Some transaction records can be retained for the limited purposes described above.
